Endless Ocean

Set sail across the ocean, discover fish, and enjoy a multiplayer fishing adventure.
Use your sonar to search the waters, cast your net, and take on fishing challenges with your crew.

  Gameplay

Explore the ocean together

Steer your boat, watch the sonar for fish, and cast your fishing net to start a fishing mini-game. Keep exploring to discover bigger challenges, including a giant fish!

-image-

  Controls

W / Up Arrow - move forward
S / Down Arrow - reverse
A / Left Arrow and D / Right Arrow - steer
Space - fish
F - toggle the spotlight
G - sound the horn
P / Escape - pause
